Telco Customer Churn Analysis

SQL | Python | Power BI | Machine Learning

Identifying customer retention risks through data.

Project Overview

This project analyzes 7,043 telecommunications customers to identify factors associated with customer churn and develop a predictive model to help identify at-risk customers.

The analysis combines exploratory data analysis, SQL-based segmentation, logistic regression, and interactive Power BI reporting.

Power BI Dashboard

Key Findings

  • Overall customer churn was approximately 26.5%, with 1,869 customers leaving.
  • Month-to-month contracts had a churn rate of approximately 42.7%, substantially higher than one-year and two-year contracts.
  • Fiber optic customers had a churn rate of approximately 41.9%.
  • Identified a high-risk segment of 589 customers with a 72.3% churn rate.
  • The logistic regression model achieved 78.42% accuracy and 0.835 ROC-AUC.

Predictive Modeling

A logistic regression model was developed using customer demographics, service usage, and account characteristics to predict customer churn.

Model performance was evaluated using accuracy, ROC-AUC, precision, recall, and a confusion matrix.

Adjusting the classification threshold from 50% to 40% increased recall from approximately 50.8% to 66.6%, allowing the model to identify more customers who were likely to churn.

Business Recommendations

  • Prioritize retention efforts for month-to-month customers, particularly those with short tenure.
  • Investigate the customer experience of fiber optic subscribers and customers without technical support.
  • Use high-risk customer segments to guide targeted retention campaigns.
  • Consider classification threshold adjustments based on the business trade-off between missed churners and unnecessary retention outreach.
